# FeralOS — The Name

## Etymology

**FeralOS** comes from the English adjective **"feral"**, derived from the Latin **"ferus"** (wild, untamed) through **"feralis"** (belonging to the wild).

In modern English, **"feral"** describes an animal that was once domesticated but has returned to a wild state — living free, self-sufficient, untamed.

## Why Feral

FeralOS is **based on Alpine Linux**. A feral animal is not a new species invented from nothing — it is the **descendant of something established, living free**. That is the relationship between FeralOS and Alpine: descended from it, yet independent.

## The Bear

The mascot is the **brown bear** (*Ursus arctos*) — the most iconic symbol of Alpine wildlife. Once extirpated from the mountains, then reintroduced through the *Life Ursus* project, the bear represents the wild returning to the Alps.

- **Feral** in the purest sense — wild, never fully domesticated
- **Powerful** — strength without excess
- **Resilient** — survives the harshest conditions
- **Self-sufficient** — autonomous, needs no permission to exist

## The Subtitle

> **FeralOS** — *Alpine Linux, Wild*

- **Alpine Linux** — the base
- **Wild** — the independence FeralOS brings
